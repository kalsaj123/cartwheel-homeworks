## Definitions

FAIL: The customer has already asked for a specific refund or cancellation to happen (not merely asked whether they are eligible, or asked about an order's status) -- and every fact the action needs (which order, the amount, the reason) is already established, typically from the order lookup -- but the reply asks the customer for permission to proceed (e.g. "want me to go ahead?", "shall I submit this?") instead of just acting and reporting the result.

PASS: Any of the following:
- The reply acts directly -- submits, queues, or completes the refund/cancellation and reports what happened -- without asking permission first.
- The customer never actually requested the refund/cancellation to happen; they only asked about eligibility or status. Asking before acting is correct here, not a failure -- there is nothing yet to act on.
- The reply asks a question that is still genuinely needed to act (a missing fact, or which of two orders is meant).
- The customer's own message explicitly invited a yes/no check (e.g. "just need a quick yes or no if you can") before wanting the action done. Answering that -- even when the answer trails into offering to proceed -- is answering what was actually asked, not an unnecessary reconfirmation. Treat this the same as a direct action.
